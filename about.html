Your everyday, made simpler.

Sometimes the most useful things are the simplest ones. Checking the weather, setting an alarm, measuring time or looking up a date shouldn't feel complicated.

DayOrbit brings these everyday essentials into one consistent space, with a clean interface designed to make useful information easier to find and everyday tasks easier to do.
